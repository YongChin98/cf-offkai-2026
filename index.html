        A 3.5-hour meetup for 180 to 200 hololive fans, one night before the big weekend.

        Comic Fiesta 2026 brings hololive Meet SUPER KARAOKE PARTY to Kuala Lumpur on 19 and 20 December, with Tokino Sora, Airani Iofifteen, Hakos Baelz, Todoroki Hajime and the four members of ASOBI★MAWARI-TAI!: Achichi Mela, Sorashina Sopia, Suzuna Tsuzuri and Hyakuto Kyoko. The Pre-Offkai warms the community up: meet fellow fans, eat, watch two cover performances, try your luck at the lucky draw, then dance through a back-to-back DJ set of hololive songs and anisong remixes.

        Everyone gets an event lanyard with the key visual, made to wear to the main event the next day.
